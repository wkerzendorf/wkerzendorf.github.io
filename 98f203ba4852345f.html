Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026.

What this covers

A private tool with a single user, who is also the person who wrote it. It runs on that person's own computer. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and it is not sold.

Data

The tool works with the Google account of the person who authorizes it, under the permissions shown on the Google consent screen at the time of authorization. What it reads stays on that person's own computer.

Nothing is sent to a server, shared with any third party, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Limited use

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account permissions page, which stops the tool reaching the account immediately.
